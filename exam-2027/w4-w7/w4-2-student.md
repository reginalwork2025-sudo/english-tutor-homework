## 10–25 分｜今日解題規則

1. however 表示轉折；therefore 表示前因後果
2. instead 表示做法替換；although 後面接子句
3. this / it / they 的答案要回前文找完整對象或事件

## 25–45 分｜文章帶讀（先限時 6 分鐘）

> A school opened a quiet study room last year. Many students were excited because they needed a place to read after class. However, some students began using the room to talk with friends. This made it difficult for others to concentrate. The school did not close the room. Instead, it divided the space into two areas: one for silent study and another for group discussions. As a result, students with different study habits could use the room comfortably. The principal said that listening to both groups had helped the school make a better decision.
