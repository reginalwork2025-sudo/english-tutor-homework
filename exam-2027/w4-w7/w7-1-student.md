## 25–45 分｜文章帶讀（先限時 6 分鐘）

> Many students think that they must study for hours to make progress. A teacher at a high school wanted to test a different idea. She invited students to read an English article for fifteen minutes each morning. They could choose topics that interested them, such as animals, science, or sports. At the end of each week, the students completed a short quiz and wrote down unfamiliar words. After six weeks, many students reported that reading had become less stressful. Some could understand short passages more quickly. The teacher stressed that this was not proof that fifteen minutes would work for everyone. Still, the project showed that a small, regular habit could make reading feel more manageable.
