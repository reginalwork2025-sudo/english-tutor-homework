## 25–45 分｜文章帶讀（先限時 6 分鐘）

> A group of students wanted to start a weekend science club. The school gave them an opportunity to design experiments and invite classmates. Their first challenge was finding a room and enough equipment. Some students worried that nobody would attend. However, the teachers offered support and allowed them to use an empty classroom. The students made a decision to begin with simple activities that needed only paper, water, and cups. Attendance began to increase gradually. As a result, the club became a regular school event and encouraged students to learn from one another.
