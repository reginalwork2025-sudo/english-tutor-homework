## 25–45 分｜文章帶讀（先限時 6 分鐘）

> When Mei started preparing for an English exam, she tried to memorize fifty new words every night. However, she forgot many of them by the next morning. Her teacher suggested a different method: learn ten words, read a short article, and use the words in sentences. At first, Mei thought the new method was too slow. After two weeks, however, she noticed that she could understand more sentences without checking a dictionary. She also spent less time reviewing old words. The experience taught her that an effective learning plan is not always the one that looks the busiest.
