## 10–25 分｜今日解題規則

1. however 引出與前句不同的觀點
2. for example 應具體支援前一個廣泛說法
3. therefore/as a result 必須前面有可推導的原因

## 25–45 分｜文章帶讀（先限時 6 分鐘）

> Online study groups are convenient because students can meet without traveling. Members can share notes and ask questions in a group chat. However, online meetings can also bring challenges. For example, some students check unrelated messages when others are speaking. To reduce distractions, one class decided to prepare three short questions before every meeting. Each member had to explain one answer aloud. As a result, the discussions became more focused. The class learned that digital tools are useful only when people use them with a clear purpose.
