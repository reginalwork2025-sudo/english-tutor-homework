## 10–25 分鐘｜規則地圖＋基礎比較

| 類別 | 你要找的線索 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| Conjunction 連接詞 | 後面常接主詞＋動詞 | although she was tired |
| Preposition 介系詞 | 後面接名詞、代名詞、動名詞等 | despite the rain / before leaving |
| Noun 名詞 | 可當主詞、受詞、介系詞受詞 | a decision; make progress |
| Verb 動詞 | 和主詞、時態或助動詞相關 | can improve / improved |
| Adjective 形容詞 | 修飾名詞或放在連綴動詞後 | a useful idea / seems useful |
| Adverb 副詞 | 常修飾動詞、形容詞、另一副詞 | read carefully / very useful |
| Suffix 字尾 | 幫助猜詞性，不能當百分百規則 | -tion(N), -ment(N), -ful(Adj), -ive(Adj), -ly(常Adv) |

**對照句：**
- Although it rained, we went out. / Despite the rain, we went out.
- Because he was ill, he stayed home. / Because of his illness, he stayed home.
- While she was studying, the phone rang. / During the lesson, the phone rang.

**迷思提醒：** friendly / lovely 是形容詞；hard = 努力地、hardly = 幾乎不；fast 可當形容詞或副詞。

## 25–40 分鐘｜Part A：連接詞／介系詞（8 題，每題 1 分）

A1. ______ she was nervous, she gave a clear speech.
(A) Although (B) Despite (C) During

(A) Although (B) Despite (C) Because

A3. Tony stayed home ______ he had a fever.
(A) because of (B) because (C) during

(A) because (B) although (C) because of

(A) while (B) during (C) although

## 40–55 分鐘｜Part B：詞性＋Suffix（10 題，每題 1 分）

## 55–70 分鐘｜Part C：整合短文（6 題，每題 1 分）

先讀整篇文章，再填空。請畫出至少三個提示你答案的單字或片語。

Many students want to improve their English reading skills. (C1) ______ they may know hundreds of words, they sometimes read slowly. One possible (C2) ______ is that they cannot recognize different word forms. For example, **decide** is a verb, while **decision** is a (C3) ______. When readers understand these patterns, they can guess the meaning of an unfamiliar word more (C4) ______. Some students study for a long time without checking their progress. (C5) ______, short quizzes can show them what they still need to practise. With regular practice, their reading speed may (C6) ______ over time.

C1. (A) Although (B) Despite (C) During

C2. (A) reason (B) reasonable (C) reasonably

C3. (A) adjective (B) noun (C) adverb

C4. (A) easy (B) ease (C) easily

C5. (A) For example (B) However (C) Because

C6. (A) improve (B) improvement (C) improved

**閱讀理解（口頭回答）：**
1. 為什麼認識很多單字的學生仍可能讀得慢？
