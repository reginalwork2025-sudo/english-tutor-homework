## 25–45 分｜文章帶讀（先限時 6 分鐘）

> During summer vacation, a community center started a reading program for teenagers. The program was available to all local students. Volunteers provided books and helped participants choose stories about science, sports, and daily life. Many teenagers had little reading experience before the program. At first, some of them found English articles difficult. However, as they discussed a short passage every day, they became more confident. By the end of the month, the program had a positive effect on the community: students shared books with friends and asked for more reading activities.
