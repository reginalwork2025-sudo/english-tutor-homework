# 學測英文 W4–W7｜8 堂課簡易教師教材

**適用：** 閱讀理解偏慢的 B 程度學生；每堂 90 分鐘，每週 2 次。每堂依序：10 分複習 → 15 分規則 → 25 分帶讀 → 20 分作答 → 15 分短寫作 → 5 分總結。每堂結束後請學生開啟 [15 分鐘課後複習](review.html)，選擇對應堂次。

> 下列短文及題目均為原創仿學測技能練習，不是正式歷屆題目。老師可以先讓學生圈出證據，再檢查答案。

## W4-1｜詞彙題：先看詞性，再看語意

**今日五字：** improve 改善(V)／method 方法(N)／similar 相似的(Adj)／reduce 減少(V)／notice 注意到(V)

**一句規則：** 空格前後先決定需要名詞、動詞或形容詞；再用前後句的意義挑選答案。

**帶讀例句：** After studying every evening, Mina noticed an improvement in her reading speed. 
- 問：noticed 是什麼詞性？improvement 是什麼詞性？
- 把句子分為原因（每天練習）與結果（閱讀速度改善）。

**自主練習：**
1. The teacher asked students to try a new ___ to remember words. (A) method (B) similar (C) reduce (D) slowly
2. Reading every day can ___ your understanding of long passages. (A) method (B) improve (C) similar (D) careful
3. These two articles are very ___ because both discuss city parks. (A) notice (B) method (C) similar (D) reduce
4. To save money, Ken decided to ___ the number of snacks he bought. (A) notice (B) reduce (C) method (D) similar

**口頭檢查：** 每題說出「空格需要什麼詞性」與「哪個字提供文意線索」。

**造句：** 用 improve 與 reduce 各造一句（不追求複雜句）。

**答案：** 1 A（new＋名詞）；2 B（can＋原形動詞）；3 C（be＋形容詞）；4 B（decided to＋原形動詞）。

## W4-2｜綜合測驗：轉折、因果、代名詞

**今日五字：** however 然而／therefore 因此／instead 反而／although 雖然／refer 指涉、提到

**一句規則：** however＝轉折；therefore＝結果；instead＝替代；代名詞 it/they 必須回頭找對應名詞。

**短文：**
Leo wanted to study with music. At first, he believed that music would help him focus. **However**, he often stopped reading to listen to songs. He decided to turn off the music **instead**. As a result, he could finish an article more quickly. **This** surprised him because he had always thought silence was boring.

**自主練習：**
1. Why did Leo stop playing music? (A) It was too expensive. (B) It distracted him. (C) He disliked songs.
2. In the last sentence, "This" mainly refers to ___. (A) finishing articles faster in silence (B) buying new songs (C) meeting friends
3. Leo had trouble focusing; ___, he changed his study habit. (A) however (B) therefore (C) although
4. Choose the closest meaning of "instead" in the passage. (A) as a replacement (B) in addition (C) at the same time

**任務：** 寫出「原本想法 → 發現問題 → 改變方法 → 結果」四格流程，畫箭頭。

**短寫作：** Although I like ___, I study better when ___.

**答案：** 1 B；2 A；3 B；4 A。注意 This 指整件結果，不一定只指前一個名詞。

## W5-1｜文意選填：先看格子詞性

**今日五字：** available 可取得的／provide 提供／effect 影響(N)／local 當地的／experience 經驗(N)

**一句規則：** 判斷空格位置；冠詞後常接名詞、be 動詞後可接形容詞，助動詞後接原形動詞。詞性不符先刪去。

**短文：**
A small town opened a reading room for students. The room was (1) ___ to everyone after school. Volunteers (2) ___ books and free learning materials. At first, only a few (3) ___ families knew about it. Later, more students came and shared their reading (4) ___. The project had a positive (5) ___ on the community.

**選項（可重複用，真實考題規則以題目說明為準）：** available / provide / effect / local / experience

**自主練習：** 完成五格，為每格寫出詞性，再解釋一個文意線索。

**短寫作：** The reading room provides ___. It has a positive effect on ___.

**答案：** 1 available (Adj)；2 provide (V；此處 volunteers 是複數)；3 local (Adj)；4 experience (N，整體經驗)；5 effect (N)。本題為教學簡化版，與正式文意選填設計未必完全一致。

## W5-2｜文意選填：詞意搭配與段落線索

**今日五字：** decision 決定／increase 增加／support 支持／opportunity 機會／challenge 挑戰

**一句規則：** 先辨詞性，再搭配常見片語：make a decision；an opportunity to；face a challenge；support someone；increase gradually。

**短文：**
When the school started a recycling club, only ten students joined. The teacher gave them an (1) ___ to design their own activities. At first, finding enough helpers was a (2) ___. Still, the students continued to (3) ___ one another. Their hard work encouraged others to join, and membership began to (4) ___. In the end, the principal made a (5) ___ to keep the club open.

**字庫：** decision / increase / support / opportunity / challenge

**自主練習：** 完成 5 格，圈出每題前後 3–5 個詞的搭配依據。

**加問：** 第三句 Still 表示什麼？為什麼不能忽略？

**短寫作：** I had an opportunity to ___. My biggest challenge was ___.

**答案：** 1 opportunity；2 challenge；3 support；4 increase；5 decision。Still 是轉折，指出即使有困難仍持續努力。

## W6-1｜篇章結構：代名詞與承接

**今日五字：** organize 組織／participant 參加者／collect 收集／community 社區／result 結果

**一句規則：** 指示詞 This/These/Such a plan 一定要有前文對應的事情；看名詞重複、時間順序及因果。

**短文：**
Every Saturday, a group of students cleaned a nearby river. At first, they collected only plastic bottles. [A] ________ The students then invited their neighbors to join. [B] ________ After two months, the riverbank looked much cleaner. [C] ________

**待填句：**
1. As a result, more than thirty community members became regular participants.
2. They soon noticed that old bags and food packages were also common.
3. This result encouraged the students to continue their work.

**自主練習：** 把 1–3 放到 A–C；每格至少指出一個線索。

**短寫作：** The students collected ___. As a result, ___.

**答案：** A→2（從只收瓶子到發現其他垃圾）；B→1（邀請鄰居→增加參加者）；C→3（this result 指河岸變乾淨）。

## W6-2｜篇章結構：轉折、原因、例子

**今日五字：** benefit 好處／reason 原因／example 例子／continue 繼續／convenient 方便的

**一句規則：** However 是反向資訊；For example 用具體例子支援前一句；Therefore 給出推論／結果。

**短文：**
Online learning is convenient because students can study from home. [A] ________ Some students find it difficult to focus when their phones are nearby. [B] ________ They may check messages during a lesson. To solve this problem, teachers can ask students to complete short tasks and explain their answers. [C] ________

**待填句：**
1. For example, they may stop listening to reply to a friend.
2. However, studying at home also creates challenges.
3. Therefore, frequent practice can help teachers check understanding.

**討論題：** A 前後是什麼關係？C 的 therefore 是根據哪個做法推出的？

**短寫作：** Online learning is convenient. However, ___.

**答案：** A→2；B→1；C→3。B 前一句談手機分心，後句用具體行為舉例。

## W7-1｜閱讀測驗：找主旨與細節

**今日五字：** habit 習慣／research 研究／daily 每天的／focus 專注／progress 進步

**一句規則：** 主旨問「整篇在說什麼」；細節問「哪一句明確寫出」。先讀題再回文定位，避免逐字翻譯。

**短文（學生先限時 6 分鐘）：**
Many students believe that studying for several hours without a break is the best way to prepare for exams. However, a shorter and more regular study routine may work better for some learners. One school encouraged students to read English for fifteen minutes every day. The students could choose short news reports, stories, or articles about their interests. After a month, several students said they could finish passages more easily. Their teacher did not claim that every student improved at the same speed. Instead, she pointed out that regular practice helped students notice unfamiliar words and return to them later. She also suggested checking progress through short quizzes rather than counting the total hours spent at a desk.

**自主練習：**
1. What is the passage mainly about? (A) Daily reading routines may help students learn. (B) All students should read only news. (C) Long study hours always work best.
2. How long did students read each day? (A) 5 minutes (B) 15 minutes (C) 50 minutes
3. What did the teacher suggest to check progress? (A) counting desk time (B) avoiding new words (C) short quizzes
4. Which statement is supported? (A) Everyone improved equally. (B) Students could choose what to read. (C) Students were not allowed to read stories.

**證據任務：** 每題在原文畫線並標 1–4。

**短寫作：** I can build a daily reading habit by ___.

**答案：** 1 A；2 B；3 C；4 B。留意 not claim 與 instead 等語意轉折。

## W7-2｜閱讀測驗：推論與作者態度

**今日五字：** suggest 建議／possible 可能的／prefer 偏好／solution 解決方案／effective 有效的

**一句規則：** 推論題只能選「文章支持的合理推斷」，不能選日常生活可能對、但本文沒說的說法；作者態度看用詞是否保留或絕對。

**短文（學生先限時 6 分鐘）：**
A city library noticed that fewer teenagers were borrowing printed books. Instead of removing its book area, the library invited students to explain how they preferred to read. Some students liked printed books because they could write notes on paper. Others preferred digital materials that could be opened on their phones. The library decided to offer both formats and started a monthly reading discussion. After three months, more teenagers visited the library, though the staff did not know which change made the greatest difference. The library's director said that listening to students was useful, but libraries should continue testing different ideas rather than expecting one perfect solution.

**自主練習：**
1. Why did the library talk to teenagers? (A) To understand their reading preferences. (B) To stop all digital reading. (C) To close the library.
2. What can be inferred about the director? (A) She believes one method works for everyone. (B) She supports trying and evaluating different approaches. (C) She dislikes student opinions.
3. Which is NOT supported by the passage? (A) More teenagers visited after three months. (B) The library offered two reading formats. (C) The discussion alone caused the increase in visitors.
4. Which best describes the author's tone? (A) balanced and practical (B) angry (C) certain that paper is superior

**證據任務：** 標記 though / rather than / both 三個關鍵訊號並解釋。

**短寫作：** I prefer reading ___ because ___.

**答案：** 1 A；2 B；3 C；4 A。沒有資料證明單一措施造成到訪人數增加，不可過度推論。

## 課後複習使用方式

1. 每堂結束請學生打開 [review.html](review.html)。
2. 選擇當天堂次，獨立完成 8 題，建議 15 分鐘內完成。
3. 完成後看「單字 /5、規則 /3」與每題解析，把成績截圖給老師；**沒有帳號登入、沒有資料上傳**。
4. 下次課開頭只複習錯的單字與規則。

**學生評量原則：** 用能否理解、回答、解釋證據評估學習；鏡頭是否開啟可作課堂約定，但不是學習成效本身。
