# 學測英文｜W1–W7 基礎規則總複習

**對象：** 閱讀較慢、已完成連接詞／介系詞／詞性／字尾與 W4–W7 題型練習的學生。

**建議時間：** 約 65–70 分鐘核心活動；另留 15–25 分鐘彈性講解與問答。先診斷，再修補錯誤，不重講整本英文文法。

## 01｜快速複習地圖（10 分鐘）

| 類別 | 規則 | 核心例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Conjunction 連接詞 | because / although / if / unless / while 後通常接子句（主詞＋動詞） | Although I was tired, I studied. |
| Preposition 介系詞 | because of / despite / during 後接名詞（片語）或動名詞 | Despite the rain, we went out. |
| Noun 名詞 | 主詞、受詞、介系詞後；a/an/the＋（形容詞）＋名詞 | This decision is important. |
| Adjective 形容詞 | 修飾名詞或放在 be/seem 等連綴動詞後 | a careful student / She is careful. |
| Adverb 副詞 | 修飾動詞、形容詞或整句；很多以 -ly 結尾但有例外 | She reads carefully. |
| Verb 動詞 | 情態助動詞後原形；to＋原形（不定詞語境）；需留意時態 | She can improve. |
| Suffix 字尾 | -tion/-ment 常為名詞；-ful/-ive 常為形容詞；-ly 常為副詞 | decide→decision; care→careful→carefully |
| 例外 | friendly / lovely 是形容詞；fast 可當副詞；hard 與 hardly 意義不同 | He works hard. |

**老師提問：** 為什麼知道一個字的詞性，有時不用知道完整中文意思就能排除選項？

## 02｜診斷 A：連接詞 vs 介系詞（15 分鐘）

選擇最佳答案，必須說明空格後面是「子句」還是「名詞片語」。

1. ___ it was raining, the game continued. (A) Although (B) Despite (C) During
2. ___ the rain, the game continued. (A) Although (B) Despite (C) Because
3. She was late ___ the heavy traffic. (A) because (B) because of (C) although
4. She was late ___ the traffic was heavy. (A) because (B) because of (C) despite
5. Please be quiet ___ the exam. (A) while (B) during (C) although
6. Please be quiet ___ others are taking the exam. (A) while (B) during (C) because of
7. You will improve ___ you practise regularly. (A) if (B) despite (C) during
8. I won't leave ___ you finish. (A) until (B) during (C) despite

**答案：** 1 A（子句）；2 B（名詞）；3 B（名詞）；4 A（子句）；5 B（the exam）；6 A（others are taking）；7 A（條件子句）；8 A（持續至某個時間點）。

## 03｜診斷 B：Noun / Adjective / Adverb / Verb（15 分鐘）

1. He gave a clear ___. (A) explain (B) explanation (C) clearly
2. She explained the rule ___. (A) clear (B) clearly (C) clarity
3. The instructions are ___. (A) clear (B) clearly (C) clarity
4. We made an important ___. (A) decide (B) decisive (C) decision
5. She is a very ___ reader. (A) care (B) careful (C) carefully
6. The number of visitors increased ___. (A) gradual (B) gradually (C) graduation
7. Students should ___ their answers. (A) check (B) checking (C) careful
8. This method is highly ___. (A) effect (B) effective (C) effectively

**答案：** 1 B（a clear＋N）；2 B（修飾 explained）；3 A（are＋Adj）；4 C（an important＋N）；5 B（a very＋Adj＋N）；6 B（修飾 increased）；7 A（should＋V 原形）；8 B（is＋Adj，highly 修飾形容詞）。

## 04｜診斷 C：Suffix 字尾與詞族（10 分鐘）

填入正確形式：
1. decide → make a ______ (decision)
2. develop → an important ______ (development)
3. create → a ______ idea (creative)
4. care → drive ______ (carefully)
5. success → a ______ student (successful)
6. educate → modern ______ (education)

**追問：** 下列敘述是對還是錯？
- 所有 -ly 結尾的字都是副詞。（錯，friendly / lovely 常為形容詞）
- 名詞前一定只能放副詞。（錯，通常用形容詞修飾名詞）
- 加了字尾後，詞性可能會改變。（對）
- hard 和 hardly 意義一樣。（錯：努力地／幾乎不）

## 05｜整合短文：一段文章用到四種規則（15 分鐘）

Many students want to improve their reading skills. (1) ___ they know many words, they may still read slowly. One reason is that they do not recognize word forms (2) ___. For example, **decide** is a verb, but **decision** is a (3) ___. Learning common suffixes can help readers understand the (4) ___ of a sentence. (5) ___ regular practice, students can gradually improve.

1. (A) Although (B) Despite (C) During
2. (A) quick (B) quickly (C) quickness
3. (A) noun (B) verb (C) adjective
4. (A) meaning (B) meaningful (C) mean
5. (A) With (B) Although (C) Because

**答案：** 1 A（後接子句）；2 B（修飾 recognize）；3 A（decision 是名詞）；4 A（the＋名詞）；5 A（with＋名詞片語）。

**理解題：**
- 第一、二句的關係是什麼？（讓步：認識很多字，也可能讀得慢）
- 本文建議了哪兩種策略？（辨認詞形、規律練習）
- 把句子 “Although they know many words, they may still read slowly.” 翻成中文，要求保留轉折意味。

## 06｜補救與離堂（10–25 分鐘，視情況）

- A 段錯 3 題以上：對比 **although vs despite / because vs because of / while vs during**，各造一句。
- B 段錯 3 題以上：在兩句短文圈出 N／V／Adj／Adv，再做 3 題詞性選擇。
- C 段錯 2 題以上：畫出 decide→decision、care→careful→carefully、success→successful 三組詞族。
- 綜合段落錯 2 題以上：請學生逐題標出語法線索，再作答一次。
- 離堂口頭題：說明 **although vs despite**、**careful vs carefully**、**decision vs decide** 三組差異。

## 評分與後續

A 8 題 + B 8 題 + C 填空 6 題 + 整合 5 題，共 27 題可計分；C 的 4 個對錯題作為口頭討論。記錄每組得分即可，**重點是找出錯誤類型**。完成這堂後，再安排下一週的學測題型訓練。
