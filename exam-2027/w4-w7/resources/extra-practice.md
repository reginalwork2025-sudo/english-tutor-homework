# W4–W7｜彈性加練題庫（每堂選做 3–5 題，約 10–15 分鐘）

**老師用法：** 原教材答完且理解後才使用；不要為了湊時間全部做完。每題先讓學生說「詞性／轉折／前後句」哪個線索決定答案。這些是原創練習，不是正式學測歷屆題目。

## W4-1 詞彙題
1. The new study plan can ___ students save time. (A) help (B) helpful (C) helpfully
2. We need a better ___ to remember words. (A) method (B) improve (C) quickly
3. The two ideas are quite ___. (A) similar (B) similarity (C) similarly
4. To protect the environment, we should ___ plastic waste. (A) reduction (B) reduce (C) reduced
5. She answered the question ___. (A) careful (B) care (C) carefully

**答案：** 1 A（can＋原形）；2 A（a better＋名詞）；3 A（are＋形容詞）；4 B（should＋原形）；5 C（修飾動詞用副詞）。

## W4-2 綜合測驗
1. The weather was bad. ___, we still went outside. (A) However (B) Therefore (C) Because
2. It rained heavily; ___, the game was canceled. (A) although (B) therefore (C) despite
3. ___ she was tired, she finished the task. (A) Despite (B) Although (C) Because of
4. I stayed home ___ the storm. (A) because (B) although (C) because of
5. Mike studied without music. **This** helped him focus. What does **This** refer to? (A) Mike's choice to study without music (B) music videos (C) his friend

**答案：** 1 A（轉折）；2 B（因果）；3 B（後接完整子句）；4 C（後接名詞片語）；5 A（指前一句行為）。

## W5-1 文意選填Ⅰ
在句子中填入 available / provide / effect / local / experience。
1. These books are ___ to all students.
2. Volunteers ___ food for visitors.
3. The program had a positive ___ on children.
4. Many ___ families came to the event.
5. She shared her travel ___ with the class.

**答案：** available / provide / effect / local / experience。請學生標明 Adj / V / N / Adj / N。

## W5-2 文意選填Ⅱ
在句子中填入 decision / increase / support / opportunity / challenge。
1. This is a good ___ to improve my English.
2. The team faced a difficult ___.
3. Friends can ___ each other.
4. The number of visitors may ___.
5. The manager made a final ___.

**答案：** opportunity / challenge / support / increase / decision。提醒搭配：an opportunity to、face a challenge、make a decision。

## W6-1 篇章結構Ⅰ
把 A–C 放入段落正確位置：
Students began cleaning the school garden. [1] ___. Next, they asked teachers for help. [2] ___. By the end of the month, flowers were growing again. [3] ___.

A. As a result, more volunteers joined the project.
B. At first, they removed old leaves and plastic bags.
C. This change made the school look brighter.

**答案：** 1 B（at first）；2 A（ask for help → volunteers）；3 C（this change 指花再度生長、景觀改善）。

## W6-2 篇章結構Ⅱ
1. The app was popular. ___, it had several problems. (A) However (B) Therefore
2. Some users found it difficult to log in. ___, they often forgot their passwords. (A) For example (B) In conclusion
3. The team simplified the login process. ___, users made fewer mistakes. (A) As a result (B) Although
4. What does **For example** usually introduce? (A) A specific example (B) An opposite idea
5. If a sentence begins with **This problem**, what should you locate? (A) A problem mentioned earlier (B) A new unrelated topic

**答案：** 1 A；2 A；3 A；4 A；5 A。

## W7-1 閱讀測驗：短文

Every Friday, the school library opens one hour earlier. Students can read quietly before class or borrow books for the weekend. According to the librarian, the change was made because many students wanted a peaceful place to study. The library also offers short reading lists for beginners.

1. Why does the library open early? (A) To help students find a quiet place (B) To sell books (C) To cancel classes
2. When does it open early? (A) Monday (B) Friday (C) Sunday
3. Who provides reading lists? (A) The library (B) A shop (C) The principal
4. What is the best title? (A) New Reading Support at School (B) Traveling Abroad (C) Weekend Sports

**答案：** 1 A；2 B；3 A；4 A。每題都要求回原文畫底線。

## W7-2 閱讀測驗：推論

Some students prefer studying alone, while others enjoy group discussions. A teacher asked both groups to try the other method for one week. Later, most students agreed that each method had strengths. The teacher concluded that learners should choose methods based on the task, rather than believing one way is always best.

1. What is the teacher's view? (A) Different tasks may need different methods (B) Group study is always best (C) Everyone must study alone
2. What can be inferred? (A) The teacher values flexibility (B) Students hate all discussions (C) The experiment lasted a year
3. Which statement is NOT supported? (A) Everyone improved their grades (B) Students tried another method (C) The methods had different strengths
4. The word **rather than** introduces ___. (A) a contrast (B) a date (C) an example

**答案：** 1 A；2 A；3 A；4 A。特別示範：不能由「覺得有效」推論「所有人成績都進步」。
