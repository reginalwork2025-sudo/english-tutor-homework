## 10–25 分｜今日解題規則

1. This / These / Such a result 必須能指回前文
2. 選句先看前一句的名詞和行為，再看後一句是否承接
3. 事件常有 first → later → finally 的順序

**老師提問：** 今天遇到看不懂的單字或空格，我可以先觀察哪些線索？

## 25–45 分｜文章帶讀（先限時 6 分鐘）

> Every month, a group of students organized a neighborhood clean-up. At first, they collected trash near the bus station. Later, the students noticed that many empty bottles were left in a nearby park. They invited local families to help. More participants joined, and the group was able to clean a larger area. After several weeks, the park looked much better. This result encouraged everyone to continue the project. The students learned that small actions can create a visible change when members of a community work together.
