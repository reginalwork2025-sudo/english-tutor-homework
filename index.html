Warm-up (spoken)

1. What's your name?
2. What do you like to do after school?
3. What's your favorite subject? Why?
4. What is difficult about learning English?

老師觀察：聽懂問題、完整回答、用字、是否願意嘗試；口說只占 5 分。

15 MIN · 15 POINTS

01 Words & Word Classes

選擇最適合的答案；最後兩題請自己寫。若你不確定，可以留空。

20 MIN · 20 POINTS

02 Sentences & Grammar

先自己作答，再向老師說明其中兩題的判斷理由。

Sentence analysis · 15 points

以下三題各 5 分，請找出句子結構或說明兩個子句的關係。

S1｜5 pts

In “My brother plays basketball after school.”, identify the subject, verb, and object.

S2｜5 pts

In “The girl is very excited.”, identify the subject, linking verb, and adjective.

S3｜5 pts

Explain how “I stayed home because I was sick.” connects two ideas. Identify the main clause and the reason clause.

20 MIN · 25 POINTS

03 Reading Comprehension

先自己閱讀，再回答問題。老師可讓學生朗讀其中 2–3 句，觀察斷句方式，但不必當作正式口說分數。

Why Is Sleep Important for Students?

Many high school students sleep late because they have a lot of homework. Some students also spend hours using their phones before bed. However, getting enough sleep is important for learning and health.

When students do not sleep well, they may feel tired in class and find it difficult to remember new information. Studies suggest that sleep helps the brain organize what we learn during the day.

To improve their sleep, students can try going to bed at the same time every night. They can also stop using their phones before bedtime. Small changes like these may help students feel more energetic and focused at school.

15 MIN · 20 POINTS

04 Writing

請用你目前會的英文完成，不需要用很難的單字。

W1｜5 pts · Make a sentence

Write one complete sentence using because.

W2｜5 pts · Combine the sentences

I was tired. I finished my homework.
Use although to combine them into one sentence.

W3｜10 pts · Short paragraph

What do you usually do after school?

Write 3–5 sentences. Use at least one conjunction (and, but, because, so, although...).

Rubrics & notes

Vocabulary · /15

V1–V10 各 1 分；V11 詞尾辨識 2 分；V12 造句 3 分。詞性或詞尾題若錯，記錄是否只是單字不認得。

Grammar · /20

G1–G10 各 2 分；選擇題以正確答案給分，另以口頭追問確認是否猜對。S1–S3 不計入本項。

Reading · /25

R1–R3 各 5 分；R4 10 分。完整答題、抓重點、使用原文或合理改寫皆可；不強制英文回答所有閱讀題。

Writing · /20

W1 /5：主從句完整 3、because 用法 2；W2 /5：although 連接正確 3、句型與標點 2；W3 /10：內容與完整性 4、句構 3、連接詞 2、可讀性 1。

Sentence structure · /15

S1：主詞 My brother 2、動詞 plays 1.5、受詞 basketball 1.5；S2：主詞 The girl 2、連綴動詞 is 1.5、形容詞 excited 1.5；S3：主句 I stayed home 2、原因子句 because I was sick 2、解釋因果關係 1。

Speaking & listening · /5

暖身口說：能理解並回應問題 2、可形成基本句子 2、能表達自身想法 1；口音不扣分。

Scores (100 points)

六大項配分與題目相對應：V1–V12（15）、S1–S3（15）、G1–G10（20）、R1–R4（25）、W1–W3（20）、暖身口說（5）。
